Getting a good photo

  • Lay the card on a plain dark surface.
  • Hold the phone 30–40 cm away and use 2× zoom. This keeps edges straight.
  • Keep the phone parallel to the card. Avoid glare and shadows.
  • Put each green handle where the two straight edges would meet, outside the rounded corner.
